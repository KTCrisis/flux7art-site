Agentic AI Governance

Before an agent runs a tool, something decides whether it may. flux7 does that in four steps: a rules engine in under a millisecond, past decisions kept in memory, an arbitration when the rules don't settle it, and a human when it matters.

Policy. Memory. Judgment. Oversight.

Four projects: one says yes or no, one remembers what was decided before, one arbitrates the unclear cases, one hands it to a human. Every decision is traced in a hash-chained log.

Music · Human × Machine

Playing piano with an AI. It listens to what I play, names the chords and the key, and answers on the instrument. It also reads finished tracks, from the audio file or straight from the Renoise project.

01 · live duet
keys7Go
Live piano ↔ AI duet

Reads chords, key and cadences live from any MIDI piano; the AI answers through the instrument, turn by turn.
02 · analysis
score7Python
Audio harmonic analysis

Key, chord grid, melody and timbre from any mp3, flac or wav. CLI and MCP, stem separation.
03 · reading
xrns7Go
Headless Renoise reader

Exact notes from .xrns files without Renoise running: song map, notes in beats, playable sequences.
